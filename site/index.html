01 / THE SHOP

A record store is
a place to belong.

Vinyl Street Co. is a Fairfield, CT record store offering new and used vinyl. This campaign is built around what makes a local shop matter: personal discovery, knowledgeable people, and the joy of finding a record you didn’t know you were looking for.

The plan focuses on reaching Fairfield-area college students and music lovers, then turning online attention into real visits and community reviews.
